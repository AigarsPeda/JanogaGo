# JāņogaGO website copy brief

Working draft, 26 September 2026. Reviewed the Latvian and English pages of the new site, the existing Janoga homepage, its lunch menu/delivery page, and Boost inc's hardware and solutions pages. This brief proposes wording and page structure; no website content has been changed.

The user confirmed that fully managed service is the main offer, with commercial details still being decided. The equipment comes from Boost inc. Cooling is the user's current understanding, so the exact machine model and food/reheating process still need confirmation.

The user has asked to retain the food images already on the new page and supplied these updated experience figures: 20 years in operation, more than 250 companies served, and more than 650 people served daily in Janoga's cafés. These describe Janoga's existing catering business, not the new vending service. The user also requested proposed answers for service scope, location suitability, and the setup process. Those sections below are draft operating commitments for Janoga to review.

## Positioning

The buyer is a business owner or someone responsible for the location: an operations manager, HR manager, facilities manager, or property operator. They need to provide food where existing options are absent or do not cover the hours people need them.

The main promise should be a practical meal service on site without having to operate a canteen. The machine is how Janoga delivers that service. Lead with the problem solved, then explain the food, responsibilities, costs, and suitability.

High foot traffic is a useful starting point, but suitability depends on likely purchases, opening hours, access, and delivery logistics. A busy corridor is not automatically a viable location. The site should invite an assessment rather than suggest every location qualifies.

The drafts below lead with the confirmed fully managed service. Public visitor locations are proposed target segments based on the brief, not verified existing installations. Confirm the operating details before publishing.

Boost inc offers smart coolers as well as other vending formats. Its hardware page describes fridge/freezer options for fresh food, snacks and drinks. This supports investigating a chilled-food service, but the supplier name alone does not identify Janoga's actual equipment or enabled features. Do not copy supplier-wide claims about payments, allergen displays, monitoring or security into Janoga's promises without confirming the installed configuration.

## What the current sites tell us

The existing site presents Janoga as a lunch restaurant in Riga that prepares meals and also offers catering. Its menu and delivery material make the food business tangible. This is the best available starting point for the new service's credibility. Explain the connection to Janoga, and confirm whether vending meals come from the same kitchen.

The new site already has useful foundations: a managed-service description, food categories, equipment options, a short setup process, FAQs, and an enquiry form. The main issues are the emphasis and missing decision information.

- The opening focuses on fresh, healthy workplace food. It should also communicate the reason a host needs the service and include visitors as well as employees.
- Equipment models appear immediately after the hero. Put the business benefit and meal offer first.
- The hero image mainly shows drinks and snacks. Use a real Janoga meal and the actual meal machine so the offer is clear.
- The FAQs repeat broad service claims. Add costs, location requirements, food handling, heating, support, and contract terms.
- The 30+ employees statement is an advertised threshold, not a verified commercial rule. Explain the demand requirement for workplaces and visitor locations separately.
- Temporary/demo equipment specifications must be replaced with verified specifications. The Necta description calls it a carousel machine while listing spirals, an inconsistency to resolve.
- The client logos do not explain which service those companies use. Distinguish existing Janoga catering or lunch clients from confirmed JāņogaGO installations.
- “Restorāna līmeņa” and “healthy” are broad quality claims. Specific food examples and an explanation of preparation and handling will do more work.

## Recommended page order and Latvian draft

### 1. Opening

Small descriptor:

> Ēdienu automāti uzņēmumiem un apmeklētāju plūsmas vietām

Headline:

> Maltītes uz vietas, bez savas ēdnīcas.

Supporting copy:

> JāņogaGO piedāvā ēdienu automātus vietām, kur darbiniekiem vai apmeklētājiem nav ērtu iespēju paēst. Pilna servisa risinājumā mēs nodrošinām automāta uzstādīšanu, maltīšu piegādi, krājumu papildināšanu un tehnisko apkopi.

Primary button:

> Saņemt piedāvājumu

Secondary button:

> Apskatīt ēdienu piedāvājumu

If the desired first step is strictly a conversation, use “Pieteikt sarunu” consistently instead. The button, form, and follow-up process must promise the same next step.

Alternative headline for a more explicit opening:

> Ēdienu automāti darbiniekiem un apmeklētājiem.

Do not make 24/7 the universal promise unless access and the meal service support it. Explain extended availability in the relevant section.

### 2. Recognisable situations and host benefits

Headline:

> Kad cilvēki ir uz vietas, bet pusdienu iespēju nav.

Introduction:

> Tuvumā nav kafejnīcas, ēdnīca nestrādā vakara maiņā vai pārtraukums ir pārāk īss, lai dotos pusdienās citur? Ēdienu automāts ļauj iegādāties maltīti turpat.

Suggested short benefit blocks:

**Maltīte turpat**

> Darbiniekiem un apmeklētājiem ir iespēja iegādāties ēdienu, nedodoties uz citu vietu.

**Bez savas virtuves un ēdnīcas komandas**

> Pilna servisa piedāvājumā Janoga nodrošina pārtikas piegādi, papildināšanu un automāta apkopi.

**Arī ārpus ierastā pusdienlaika**

> Ēdienu var iegādāties laikā, kad automāts ir pieejams jūsu darbiniekiem un apmeklētājiem.

The last block deliberately ties availability to access. Do not imply overnight support or guaranteed stock at all times.

### 3. Food and preparation

Introduction:

> Apskatiet maltīšu piemērus un pārrunājiet jūsu atrašanās vietai piemērotu sortimentu.

Keep the existing page photos for now, as requested. Present them as illustrations of the food categories, rather than exact product photography. Use real product photos, portion sizes and current vending prices when available. Avoid copying the restaurant's lunch prices onto the vending offer.

Updated introduction:

> Ēdieni, uzkodas un dzērieni ikdienas maltītēm un pauzēm. Sortimentu izvēlamies atbilstoši jūsu darbinieku un apmeklētāju vajadzībām.

Keep three image cards with the client's requested categories, left to right. Use the existing salad-bowl photo first, the sandwich/hotdog photo in the middle, and the doughnuts on the right.

**Pamatēdieni un salāti**

> Pilnvērtīga maltīte pusdienu pauzei.

**Sendviči un uzkodas**

> Arī konditoreja nelielai pauzei.

**Deserti un dzērieni**

> Saldai pauzei.

Small note below the cards:

> Attēli ilustrē ēdienu kategorijas. Faktiskais sortiments var atšķirties.

The client confirmed the category grouping above. Confirm the individual products in the launch assortment separately.

The food section needs one plain sentence explaining heating. Choose the version that matches the actual service:

- Chilled food with separate reheating: “Maltītes automātā tiek uzglabātas atdzesētas. Pirms ēšanas tās var uzsildīt [apstiprināta uzsildīšanas iespēja].”
- Heating inside the machine: “Izvēlēto maltīti automāts uzsilda pirms izsniegšanas.”

Add confirmed information about food dates, storage, ingredients, allergens, and dietary choices. Do not imply that chilled prepared meals are dispensed hot or promise daily cooking/refilling without an agreed process.

### 4. Janoga's food experience

Headline:

> Jāņogas pieredze maltīšu gatavošanā.

Updated copy using the user's supplied figures:

> Ikdienā gatavojam un pasniedzam maltītes uzņēmumu darbiniekiem un mūsu kafejnīcu apmeklētājiem. Ar JāņogaGO šo pieredzi izmantojam, lai nodrošinātu maltītes arī vietās, kur nav kafejnīcas vai tuvumā pieejamu pusdienu iespēju.

Suggested figures displayed next to the copy:

- **20 gadi**. Pieredze ēdināšanā.
- **250+ uzņēmumu**. Apkalpoti šo gadu laikā.
- **650+ cilvēku**. Ik dienu paēd mūsu kafejnīcās.

These figures must remain explicitly attached to Janoga's food business. Do not label them vending customers, installations, or vending users.

If confirmed, a stronger and more specific headline is “Maltītes no Jāņogas virtuves.” Show the kitchen, team, or actual meals to substantiate it.

Use customer logos with a precise explanation of the relationship. If they are existing catering customers, “Mūsu ēdināšanas klienti” is clearer than implying that all have vending installations. Add a vending case study once one is available: location type, people served, service scope, and an approved customer quote.

### 5. Suitable locations

Headline:

> Vai ēdienu automāts derēs jūsu atrašanās vietai?

Copy:

> Ja jūsu atrašanās vietā ikdienā strādā vai uzturas cilvēki, kuriem nav ērtu iespēju paēst, ēdienu automāts var būt piemērots risinājums. Tas var noderēt ražotnēs, noliktavās, biroju ēkās un vietās ar regulāru apmeklētāju plūsmu, arī tad, ja cilvēki tur uzturas vakaros vai strādā maiņās.
>
> Pirms piedāvājuma sagatavošanas izvērtējam cilvēku plūsmu, paredzamo pieprasījumu, darba laiku un vietu automātam. Pārbaudām arī iespējas nodrošināt piegādes un regulāru apkalpošanu. Pastāstiet par savu atrašanās vietu, un kopā izvērtēsim piemērotāko risinājumu.

Suggested examples to include after confirming operational suitability:

- Ražotnes un noliktavas, īpaši ar maiņu darbu.
- Loģistikas centri un biroju ēkas.
- Apmeklētāju vietas, piemēram, izglītības iestādes, sporta centri un uzgaidāmās telpas.

Publish the actual service territory and basic installation conditions here, not only in a sales call. Distinguish indoor installations from any supported outdoor use. If there is a minimum demand requirement, state how it is assessed.

### 6. Service responsibilities and costs

Headline:

> Kas ietilpst servisā, un kas jānodrošina jums?

Proposed managed-service wording, requested by the user. This assumes Janoga includes routine machine cleaning and the host supplies space, power and service access. Confirm those responsibilities before publication.

> JāņogaGO rūpējas par automāta uzstādīšanu, maltīšu piegādi, krājumu papildināšanu, tīrīšanu un tehnisko apkopi. Vienojamies par sortimentu un apkalpošanas kārtību, lai ēdienu piedāvājums atbilstu jūsu atrašanās vietas vajadzībām.
>
> Jums jānodrošina piemērota vieta automātam, elektrības pieslēgums un piekļuve mūsu komandai piegāžu un apkopes laikā. Uzstādīšanas prasības, pienākumu sadalījumu un izmaksas precizējam piedāvājumā pirms sadarbības sākuma.
>
> Jūsu darbiniekiem nav jāorganizē maltīšu piegādes vai jāpapildina automāta krājumi.

Optional two-column presentation:

| Mēs nodrošinām | Jūs nodrošināt |
| --- | --- |
| Automāta uzstādīšanu | Piemērotu vietu automātam |
| Maltīšu piegādi un krājumu papildināšanu | Elektrības pieslēgumu |
| Automāta tīrīšanu un tehnisko apkopi | Piekļuvi piegādēm un apkopei |

Include a simple responsibility table covering installation, electricity, connectivity, food delivery, cleaning, stock/date checks, maintenance, and support. Name the responsible party for each. Do not assume “full service” answers those questions.

Publish the host's commercial model: purchase or rental, installation fees, monthly charges, minimum purchases, and who pays for the food. If pricing varies, explain what determines it and what a quote includes. Do not use “free installation,” “no investment,” or “no costs” unless those statements accurately describe the whole arrangement.

Remove the equal-weight equipment-rental offer from the main page. The confirmed main service gives Janoga responsibility for operating the meal service. Asking a buyer to choose between that and managing the food themselves distracts from the offer. Add a separate rental page later only if rental becomes a confirmed service.

### 7. Setup process

Headline:

> Kā sākt sadarbību?

1. **Pastāstiet par savu atrašanās vietu.** Aizpildiet pieteikumu vai sazinieties ar mums. Norādiet pilsētu, aptuveno darbinieku vai apmeklētāju skaitu un laiku, kad nepieciešama iespēja paēst.
2. **Sagatavojam piedāvājumu.** Pārrunājam jūsu vajadzības un izvērtējam uzstādīšanas iespējas. Piedāvājumā iekļaujam piemērotu automātu, sortimentu, servisa nosacījumus un izmaksas.
3. **Uzstādām un apkalpojam.** Pēc vienošanās uzstādām automātu un nodrošinām pirmo pārtikas piegādi. Turpmāk rūpējamies par krājumu papildināšanu un automāta apkopi.

Button:

> Saņemt piedāvājumu

Add a launch timeframe only once one can be reliably promised.

### 8. Equipment details

Headline:

> Automāts jūsu telpai un cilvēku plūsmai.

Supporting line:

> Automāta izvēli pārrunājam atbilstoši sortimentam, pieprasījumam un pieejamajai vietai.

Replace the current Boostic/Necta model cards and demo specifications with information about the actual Boost inc equipment Janoga will install. Confirm the product name rather than treating “Boostic” as a verified model. Publish verified dimensions, capacity, power/connection requirements, payment options, temperature handling, and any heating capability. These details help a host decide whether installation is feasible. Move other technical specifications into expandable details or a downloadable sheet.

If the equipment is a smart cooler rather than a traditional dispensing machine, explain the purchase process in customer language. Do not write “automāts izsniedz maltīti” until the actual mechanism is known. A suitable generic headline is “Izvēlieties maltīti un iegādājieties to uz vietas.”

### 9. FAQs

Prioritise these questions and publish actual answers:

1. Kādas ir uzstādīšanas un ikmēneša izmaksas?
2. Kādam cilvēku skaitam vai pieprasījumam automāts ir piemērots?
3. Kurās pilsētās nodrošināt servisu?
4. Cik daudz vietas nepieciešams, un kādi pieslēgumi jānodrošina?
5. Kā tiek uzglabātas un uzsildītas maltītes?
6. Kādas ir maltīšu cenas un norēķinu iespējas?
7. Cik bieži automātu papildina, un kas notiek ar nepārdoto pārtiku?
8. Kas notiek, ja automāts nedarbojas vai pirkums neizdodas?
9. Vai uzņēmums var apmaksāt daļu no darbinieku maltītēm?
10. Kāds ir līguma termiņš, un vai iespējams izmēģinājums?

Do not invent answers to fill the section. Costs, suitability, territory, and installation requirements should also appear in the main page content because they are basic buying information.

### 10. Enquiry

Headline:

> Pārrunāsim ēdināšanu jūsu atrašanās vietā.

Copy:

> Pastāstiet, kur nepieciešams ēdienu automāts, cik cilvēku varētu to izmantot un kādā laikā. Sazināsimies, lai pārrunātu piemērotību un sagatavotu piedāvājumu.

Suggested fields:

- Uzņēmums
- Kontaktpersona
- E-pasts
- Atrašanās vieta vai pilsēta
- Aptuvenais lietotāju skaits dienā
- Papildu informācija, optional
- Tālrunis, optional if email is sufficient

Keep the enquiry short. Ask about shifts, visitor patterns, and exact installation details during follow-up or make them optional. Replace the employee-only field with wording that also fits visitor locations. Clearly distinguish total employees/visitors from expected buyers when assessing the enquiry.

Button:

> Pieprasīt piedāvājumu

Proposed confirmation message, only when the form has actually recorded the enquiry:

> Paldies! Esam saņēmuši jūsu pieprasījumu. Sazināsimies ar jums, lai pārrunātu atrašanās vietu un nākamos soļus.

Do not add a response-time promise without an agreed service standard. Use a confirmed, monitored contact address consistently across pages.

## Details needed before final publication

Confirm service territory; minimum demand; host costs and contract terms; meal prices; kitchen/source of the food; actual Boost inc model and purchase process; chilled storage and reheating arrangements; installation requirements; restocking, cleaning and date-control responsibilities; support hours and failed-purchase process; payment methods; subsidy options; verified equipment specifications; customer relationships behind the logos; and the contact mailbox.

The immediate priority is to settle the commercial offer, food/heating process, and eligibility. Those facts will make the page more persuasive than adding more adjectives to the headline.
